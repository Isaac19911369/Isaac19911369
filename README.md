# Dr. Eshagh Dehmollaian

**Senior AI & Data Specialist** · PhD in Electronics & Information Technology · Linz, Austria

I design, build and run AI systems that have to work in production — from deep-learning anomaly detection on IoT data to enterprise AI assistants deployed on Kubernetes. My background combines a PhD in AI-driven wireless security, six years of applied ML research at JKU Linz, and hands-on telecom engineering at Huawei.

- **Generative AI & AI assistants** — RAG systems, function calling, AI agents and multi-agent architectures, fine-tuning, LLM/VLM integration into enterprise backends
- **AI infrastructure & MLOps** — model serving with NVIDIA NIM / Triton, Kubernetes & Helm, CI/CD, MLflow, hybrid cloud / on-premise deployments
- **Time series & anomaly detection** — deep-learning anomaly detection in large-scale IoT systems, forecasting and predictive maintenance
- **Applied research** — ML for wireless communication and physical-layer security, centralized and decentralized (federated) learning
